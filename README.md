Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
Project Overview
This project uses SQL and PostgreSQL to analyze coffee shop sales data and explore business performance.
Tools Used
•	PostgreSQL
•	pgAdmin 4
Business Questions
•	Total Revenue
•	Total orders
•	Average order value
•	Total pizzas sold
•	Average Pizzas per Order
•	Monthly Total Revenue Trend
•	Percentage of sales by pizza size
•	Revenue by pizza name
•	Top pizza by Revenue
•	Top pizza by quantity
•	Top pizza by orders
SQL Skills
•	Joins
•	GROUP BY
•	Aggregate functions such as SUM and COUNT
•	Date filtering and EXTRACT
•	COUNT(DISTINCT)

Project Files
•	SQL file: Queries used to analyze coffee shop sales data.
Key Findings
1.	The Revenue trend shows September sold the most pizzas
2.	The daily Revenue trend shows Friday is the busiest day of the week
3.	Looking at percentage of sales by pizza category Supreme has the biggest percentage 
4.	Looking at the total Revenue by pizza name The Thai chicken pizza generated the most Revenue. The worst is the Brie Carre pizza.
